Witness Builder

Builds the 13-input witness for the GatekeeperEvaluation circuit, validates the decision rules, and previews the publicSignals layout.

Honest scope: this tool builds witness JSON and validates inputs. It does NOT generate proofs, it does not verify proofs, and the publicSignals preview proves nothing by itself. A real proof still needs the official circuit artifacts and a prover (e.g. snarkjs groth16.fullProve).
